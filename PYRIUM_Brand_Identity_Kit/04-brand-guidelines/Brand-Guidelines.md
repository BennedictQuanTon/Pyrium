# PYRIUM — Brand Asset Kit

## Brand positioning
**Private AI. Built to act.**  
Supporting line: **Your data. Your models. Your control.**  
Logo descriptor in supplied artwork: **PRIVATE AI. REAL-WORLD IMPACT.**

## Core palette
| Token | Hex | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Black | `#111111` | Wordmark, headings, dark surfaces |
| Signal Red | `#D9292F` | Primary accent, active states, CTAs |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main background |
| Graphite | `#8A8A8A` | Secondary text and dividers |
| Mist | `#F3F3F3` | Subtle surfaces |

## Typography
- Display: Inter Display / Inter
- Body: Inter
- Avoid JetBrains Mono as a primary brand font; use a neutral system sans for technical labels when needed.

## Logo use
- Use transparent primary logo on white/light backgrounds.
- Use reversed logo on dark backgrounds.
- Keep aspect ratio; do not stretch, skew, rotate, or add effects.
- Leave clear space around the lockup at least the height of the red dot in the wordmark.
- Use mark-only assets for compact app and avatar contexts.
- At small sizes, prefer the mark over the full lockup.

## Asset accuracy note
`PYRIUM-Primary-Exact.svg` embeds the supplied raster artwork inside SVG to preserve its appearance. `PYRIUM-Primary-Vector-Trace.svg` is an editable trace and may differ slightly from the source raster. For exact typography, use the exact artwork asset; for editable production artwork, refine the vector trace in a vector editor.
